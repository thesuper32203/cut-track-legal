Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 12, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Cut Track ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application Cut Track (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address and a unique identifier from that provider. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Profile and health data: Information you voluntarily enter, including age, sex, height, weight, activity level, and dietary goals.
  • Food and nutrition logs: Meals, food items, and macro/calorie entries you log within the App.
  • Subscription and purchase data: Information about your in-app purchases and subscription status, managed through RevenueCat. We do not store your payment card information.
  • Device information: Basic device identifiers and operating system version used for app functionality and debugging.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App and its features
  • Calculate and display personalized macro and calorie targets
  • Sync your data across sessions and devices
  • Process and verify in-app subscriptions
  • Respond to your support requests or questions
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve app performance

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Apple HealthKit

With your permission, the App may read the following data from Apple HealthKit: steps, active energy burned, and calories burned. This data is used solely to provide more accurate calorie and macro tracking within the App.

  • We do not use HealthKit data for advertising or other use-based data mining purposes.
  • We do not share HealthKit data with third parties except as required to provide the App's core features (e.g., storing your data in our database).
  • HealthKit data is never sold or used for any purpose unrelated to health and fitness.

You can revoke HealthKit access at any time in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

4. Camera and Barcode Scanning

The App requests access to your device camera to enable barcode scanning and meal photo analysis. Camera data is processed on-device or via our servers solely to identify food items and retrieve nutrition information. We do not store raw camera images beyond the duration of a single scan session.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Supabase — backend database and authentication infrastructure. Your data is stored on Supabase servers. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • RevenueCat — subscription and purchase management. See revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Apple Sign In / Google Sign In — third-party authentication. Governed by Apple's and Google's respective privacy policies.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers provided by Supabase. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the App's services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at cuttrackofficial@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you reside, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete your information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at cuttrackofficial@gmail.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
